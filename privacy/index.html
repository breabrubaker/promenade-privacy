Promenade

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Promenade is a personal walking journal. Your privacy is simple to explain: everything you record stays on your device.

What Promenade collects

Nothing. Promenade has no servers of its own, no accounts to create, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking of any kind. Your walks, photos, notes, routes, stamps, and the names of anyone you note you walked with are stored in the app's local database on your iPhone, and nothing is ever sent to the developer. One thing reaches Apple rather than the developer: drawing maps, explained under Maps and place names. Promenade does not copy your journal to iCloud.

Location

With your permission, Promenade uses your location to record walk routes, to show your own walks on a map in your journal, and to work out which of the app's suggested walks are near you. Your routes are stored on your device, and in Apple Health if you let Promenade save walks there. Promenade never uploads them, and no one, including the developer, can see where you have walked. Finding walks near you happens entirely on your device: your location is compared against the walks already built into the app, and is never sent anywhere.

Maps and place names

Promenade shows maps using Apple Maps, part of iOS. Like any app that displays a map, this means some information reaches Apple in order to draw it:

  • Showing a map sends the area being displayed to Apple, so the map images can be fetched.
  • Searching for a location sends what you typed.
  • Turning a walk's coordinates into a place name (for your passport stamps, or the label on a saved walk) sends those coordinates.

These requests are handled by Apple Maps, not by Promenade. Apple states that Maps requests are not associated with your Apple ID, and Promenade receives nothing back about you. Your walks, photos, notes, and recorded routes are never included in them, and Promenade has no server of its own to send anything to. Apple explains its own handling in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Apple Health

With your permission, Promenade can read walking workouts from Apple Health to import them into your journal, and can save walks you record back to Apple Health as a walking workout with its route, distance, and an estimated calorie count. Health data is used only inside the app on your device and is never transmitted or shared.

Motion & fitness

With your permission, Promenade uses your iPhone's motion sensor to count the steps you take during a walk you record. The step count is saved with that walk on your device and is never sent anywhere. If you decline, everything else about recording a walk still works. The step count is simply left blank for you to fill in yourself.

Photos

Promenade lets you attach photos from your library to your walks. Choosing them uses Apple's own photo picker, which hands Promenade only the pictures you pick. The app sees nothing else, and no permission is needed for this.

Separately, if you choose "From my photos" when logging a walk, Promenade asks permission to look through your photo library and suggest walks you may have taken. It reads only when and where each photo was taken, groups photos that were taken near each other around the same time, and shows you those as suggestions. Nothing is added to your journal until you pick one, and only then are the pictures themselves read. Screenshots are skipped. This all happens on your device. Your photos and their locations are never uploaded, and Promenade has no server to send them to. You can decline, and everything else about logging a walk still works; you can also change your mind later in Settings.

Photos you add to a walk (and their embedded location, when present, to place walks on the map) stay on your device.

Sharing

If you share a postcard or Keepsake Book, you choose exactly what is shared and with whom through the standard iOS share sheet. Promenade itself sends nothing.

A Keepsake Book can be made ready for a print service, such as Lulu, Shutterfly, Snapfish or Walmart, as a PDF or as a picture of each page. You send it to them yourself, through the share sheet or on their own website. Promenade never contacts a print service, and what you upload there is handled under that service's own privacy policy.

Backing up your journal

Settings offers a backup file containing your whole journal: walks, photos, notes, routes, who you walked with, stamps, keepsakes, and which suggested walks you've bookmarked. It is written only when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes through the standard iOS share sheet: saved to Files, kept in iCloud Drive, or sent to another device. Promenade does not upload it anywhere and keeps no copy beyond the one on your device.

The file is readable by anyone who has it, so treat it like the journal itself and keep it somewhere you're comfortable with. Restoring from a backup only adds what isn't already in the app; it never deletes anything you've written since.

Data deletion

Deleting a walk removes it from the app, though any stamps it earned stay in your passport until you remove them. Deleting the app removes all app data from your device. Walks saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. Health permissions can be managed at any time in the iOS Health app, and location, photo, and motion permissions in Settings.

Children

Promenade does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Promenade changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated first and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
