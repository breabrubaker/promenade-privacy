Promenade Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Promenade is a personal walking journal. Your privacy is simple to explain: everything you record stays on your device.

What Promenade collects

Nothing. Promenade has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking of any kind. Your walks, photos, notes, routes, and stamps are stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone — there is no other copy, and nowhere else for them to go. The app does draw maps using Apple Maps, which is built into iOS; what that involves is explained under Maps and place names below.

Location

With your permission, Promenade uses your location to record walk routes and show your walks on a map. Your routes are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, and no one — including the developer — can see where you have walked.

Maps and place names

Promenade shows maps using Apple Maps, part of iOS. Like any app that displays a map, this means some information reaches Apple in order to draw it:

  • Showing a map sends the area being displayed to Apple, so the map images can be fetched.
  • Searching for a location sends what you typed.
  • Turning a walk's coordinates into a place name — for your passport stamps, or the label on a saved walk — sends those coordinates.

These requests are handled by Apple Maps, not by Promenade. Apple states that Maps requests are not associated with your Apple ID, and Promenade receives nothing back about you. Your walks, photos, notes, and recorded routes are never included in them, and Promenade has no server of its own to send anything to. Apple explains its own handling in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Apple Health

With your permission, Promenade can read walking workouts from Apple Health to import them into your journal, and can save walks you record back to Apple Health. Health data is used only inside the app on your device and is never transmitted or shared.

Motion & fitness

With your permission, Promenade uses your iPhone's motion sensor to count the steps you take during a walk you record. The step count is saved with that walk on your device and is never sent anywhere. If you decline, everything else about recording a walk still works — the step count is simply left blank for you to fill in yourself.

Photos

With your permission, Promenade lets you attach photos from your library to your walks. Photos (and their embedded location, when present, to place walks on the map) stay on your device.

Sharing

If you share a postcard or keepsake book, you choose exactly what is shared and with whom through the standard iOS share sheet. Promenade itself sends nothing.

Data deletion

Deleting a walk removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes all app data from your device. Health permissions can be managed at any time in the iOS Health app, and location, photo, and motion permissions in Settings.

Children

Promenade does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Promenade changes how data is handled (for example, optional iCloud sync), this policy will be updated first and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
