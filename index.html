Promenade Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Promenade is a personal walking journal. Your privacy is simple to explain: everything you record stays on your device.

What Promenade collects

Nothing. Promenade has no servers of its own, no accounts to create, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking of any kind. Your walks, photos, notes, routes, and stamps are stored in the app's local database on your iPhone, and nothing is ever sent to the developer. Two things reach Apple rather than the developer: drawing maps, explained under Maps and place names, and — only if you switch it on — iCloud sync.

Location

With your permission, Promenade uses your location to record walk routes, to show your own walks on a map in your journal, and to work out which of the app's suggested walks are near you. Your routes are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, and no one — including the developer — can see where you have walked. Finding walks near you happens entirely on your device: your location is compared against the walks already built into the app, and is never sent anywhere.

Maps and place names

Promenade shows maps using Apple Maps, part of iOS. Like any app that displays a map, this means some information reaches Apple in order to draw it:

  • Showing a map sends the area being displayed to Apple, so the map images can be fetched.
  • Searching for a location sends what you typed.
  • Turning a walk's coordinates into a place name — for your passport stamps, or the label on a saved walk — sends those coordinates.

These requests are handled by Apple Maps, not by Promenade. Apple states that Maps requests are not associated with your Apple ID, and Promenade receives nothing back about you. Your walks, photos, notes, and recorded routes are never included in them, and Promenade has no server of its own to send anything to. Apple explains its own handling in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Apple Health

With your permission, Promenade can read walking workouts from Apple Health to import them into your journal, and can save walks you record back to Apple Health. Health data is used only inside the app on your device and is never transmitted or shared.

Motion & fitness

With your permission, Promenade uses your iPhone's motion sensor to count the steps you take during a walk you record. The step count is saved with that walk on your device and is never sent anywhere. If you decline, everything else about recording a walk still works — the step count is simply left blank for you to fill in yourself.

Photos

With your permission, Promenade lets you attach photos from your library to your walks. Photos (and their embedded location, when present, to place walks on the map) stay on your device.

Sharing

If you share a postcard or keepsake book, you choose exactly what is shared and with whom through the standard iOS share sheet. Promenade itself sends nothing.

iCloud sync

Off unless you turn it on. Promenade never syncs by default; the switch lives in Settings → Back up & restore, and until you use it your journal stays on your iPhone.

With it on, your journal is kept in your own iCloud account, in what Apple calls a private database. That means:

  • The developer cannot see it. Private iCloud data isn't visible to app developers, including in Apple's own tools — there is no dashboard anywhere showing your walks.
  • It uses your iCloud storage, not a service Promenade pays for, so a large journal counts against your iCloud plan.
  • Your devices are told when something has changed using silent push notifications. These are invisible, carry no content, and never appear as an alert.
  • Turning sync off stops any further syncing. The copy already in your iCloud account stays there until you remove it, which you can do from the iOS Settings app under your name → iCloud.

Even with sync on, nothing is sent to the developer and there is still no Promenade server. Apple explains how it handles iCloud data in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Backing up your journal

Settings offers a backup file containing your whole journal — walks, photos, notes, routes, stamps and keepsakes. It is written only when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes through the standard iOS share sheet: saved to Files, kept in iCloud Drive, or sent to another device. Promenade does not upload it anywhere and keeps no copy beyond the one on your device.

The file is readable by anyone who has it, so treat it like the journal itself — keep it somewhere you're comfortable with. Restoring from a backup only adds walks that aren't already in the app; it never deletes anything you've written since.

Data deletion

Deleting a walk removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes all app data from your device. Health permissions can be managed at any time in the iOS Health app, and location, photo, and motion permissions in Settings.

Children

Promenade does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Promenade changes how data is handled (for example, optional iCloud sync), this policy will be updated first and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
